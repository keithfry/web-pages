Large language model (LLM)-based systems are deployed to conduct scientific research autonomously but whether their reasoning adheres to epistemic norms is poorly understood. An evaluation of LLM-based agents across eight domains found that the base model accounts for most explained variance and determines both performance and behavior. Evidence is ignored in 68% of traces, refutation-driven belief revision occurs in 26%, and convergent multi-test evidence is rare. This lack of epistemic patterns suggests that current LLM-based agents do not exhibit scientific reasoning.
