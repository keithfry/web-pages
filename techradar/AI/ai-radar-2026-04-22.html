On Solving the Multiple Variable Gapped Longest Common Subsequence Problem

A paper addresses the Variable Gapped Longest Common Subsequence (VGLCS) problem, a generalization of the classical LCS problem involving flexible gap constraints between consecutive solutions' characters. A new search framework is proposed based on a root-based state graph representation and an iterative beam search strategy to cope with the resulting combinatorial explosion. The approach exploits known heuristics from the LCS literature and has been tested on 320 synthetic instances with up to 10 input sequences and 500 characters, showing robustness over a baseline beam search in comparable runtimes.

ARES (Adaptive Red-Teaming and End-to-End Repair of Policy-Reward System) is a framework that systematically discovers and mitigates dual vulnerabilities in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Reward Models (RMs). HALO: Hybrid Auto-encoded Locomotion with Learned Latent Dynamics, Poincar\'e Maps, and Regions of Attraction

HALO (Hybrid Auto-encoded Locomotion) is a framework for learning latent reduced-order models of periodic hybrid dynamics directly from trajectory data. It employs an autoencoder to identify a low-dimensional latent state and a learned latent Poincaré map that captures step-to-step locomotion dynamics, enabling Lyapunov analysis and the construction of a region of attraction in the latent space. This allows for stability structure and prediction of full-order region-of-attraction boundaries to be transferred back to the full-order system. Experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of HALO on simulated hopping robots and full-body humanoid locomotion.
