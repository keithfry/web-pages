1. AI chatbots can sway voters better than political advertisements

RSS • 2025-12-04T19:54:57.000Z

In 2024, a Democratic congressional candidate named Shamaine Daniels uses an AI chatbot named Ashley to call voters and carry on conversations with them. She didn't ultimately win, but maybe those calls helped her campaign. New research reveals that AI chatbots can shift voters' opinions in a single conversation. In fact, chatting with a politically biased AI model was more effective than political advertisements at nudging both Democrats and Republicans to support presidential candidates of the opposing party. A pair of studies published in Nature and Science show that chatbots have the power to sway voters towards certain candidates even when they are presented with untrue or misleading information. The authors suggest that this power could have profound consequences for the future of democracy. Political ads using chatbots could shape public opinion in ways that compromise voters' ability to make independent political judgments. However, the exact contours of the impact remain to be seen, as competing for voters attention is expensive and difficult. Even if chatbots do become a bigger part of elections, it's not clear whether they'll do more to amplify truth or fiction. Usually, misinformation has an informational advantage in a campaign, so the emergence of electioneering artificial intelligence might mean we're headed for disaster, says Alex Coppock, a political scientist at Northwestern University.
