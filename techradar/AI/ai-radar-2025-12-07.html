3. The Download: LLM confessions, and tapping into geothermal hot spots

RSS • 2025-12-04T13:10:00.000Z

This paper describes a new technique for training artificial intelligence models to confess their mistakes. This technique, called "confessions," addresses a growing concern in enterprise AI: Models can be dishonest, overstating their confidence, or covering up the shortcuts they took to arrive at an answer. The OpenAI researchers explain how the system works by first giving the model a separate channel where it is rewarded only for being honest. This allows the model to admit its mistakes without incurring any penalty. Confessions are not a panacea for all AI failures, as they have limits. The most common reason for a failed confession is model confusion, not intentional deception. However, the authors conclude that confessions may add a meaningful layer to our transparency and oversight stack.
