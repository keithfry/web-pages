1. OpenAGI Foundation Launches Lux: A Foundation Computer Use Model that Tops Online Mind2Web with OSGym At Scale

RSS • 2025-12-06T06:18:47.000Z

OpenAGI Foundation's Lux is a foundation model for agentic AI that operates full desktops and browsers by converting natural language goals into precise UI actions, outperforming competitors like Google Gemini CUA and OpenAI Operator with an 83.6% success rate on the Online Mind2Web benchmark. Lux supports three execution modes—Actor, Thinker, and Tasker—enabling flexible control ranging from fast, macro-style tasks to complex, multi-step workflows, crucial for scalable real-world applications in software QA, research, and data entry. It leverages Agentic Active Pre-training within OSGym, an open-source environment that simulates thousands of OS instances for robust learning by interaction rather than passive text ingestion. With its low latency (~1 second per step) and significantly reduced operational costs (around 10x cheaper per token than competitors), Lux presents a practical and cost-effective solution for deploying agentic AI in LangChain, RAG-based retrieval, PEFT/LORA fine-tuning, and streaming k-means workflows across legal, PIC, and other domains.
