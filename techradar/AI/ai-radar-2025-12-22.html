3. AI Interview Series #4: Explain KV Caching

RSS • 2025-12-21T09:23:44.000Z

The article explains KV caching, a key optimization in autoregressive large language model (LLM) inference that addresses the slowdown in token generation as sequences grow longer. Without KV caching, the model recomputes attention over all previous tokens at each step, causing quadratic computation growth and significant latency. KV caching stores and reuses keys and values from prior tokens, reducing redundant computations and enabling near-linear scaling of generation time, which is critical for efficient production deployment of LLMs. This optimization directly impacts topics like LLM inference efficiency, agentic AI responsiveness, RAG systems, and frameworks like LangChain that rely on fast, scalable token generation.
