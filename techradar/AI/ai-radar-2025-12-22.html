2. AI Interview Series #4: Explain KV Caching

RSS • 2025-12-21T09:23:44.000Z

The article explains KV caching, a critical optimization for large language model (LLM) inference that stores and reuses keys and values from earlier tokens to avoid redundant computation during autoregressive token generation. Without KV caching, inference time grows quadratically with sequence length due to recomputing attention over all previous tokens; enabling KV caching reduces this to near-linear growth, drastically speeding up generation—demonstrated as nearly a 5× speedup in a GPT-2-medium benchmark. This matters for LLM deployment, Agentic AI, RAG, and tooling like LangChain or Ollama, where efficient, scalable token generation is essential for performance and cost-effectiveness in production systems.
