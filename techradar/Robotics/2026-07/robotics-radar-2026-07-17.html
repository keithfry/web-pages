IEEE Spectrum - Robotics

How to Make an Invisible Drone

Northwestern University roboticists demonstrated Phantom Twist, a drone designed for near-invisibility using computational design. The drone achieves its stealth by spinning rapidly (15 to 25 Hz), causing motion blur that leverages the human eye's "persistence of vision." This technique requires careful structural design so components align with empty space as it spins. The optimization process used a metric minimizing perceived image differences between background and drone overlays, achieving an LPIPS score far better than human-designed versions. While currently requiring an optical tracking system, future applications could include covert surveillance or 360-degree wildlife observation.

Adaptive Control of Motor-Position-Controlled Flexible Joint Robots with Uncertain Joint Stiffness

Model-based control of flexible joint robots using actuators that control position requires knowing the precise joint stiffness, which is often uncertain or changes over time due to wear. To address this, the paper proposes an adaptive control method to continuously estimate the unknown, nonlinear torque-deflection relationship at each joint. Unlike methods for non-elastic robots, this approach utilizes an implicit control law and a regressor matrix dependent on the control input. The proposed technique accounts for the uncertainties introduced by variable joint stiffness. Experimental validation on a flexible joint with non-linear stiffness confirms the effectiveness of this adaptive strategy.

Introduces a novel multi-modal framework for autonomous humanoid control driven by semantic audio input. Music input uses audio fingerprinting to map musical segments to appropriate motion policies. Speech input grounds direct human-robot interaction within a library of imitation-learned skills. Open-AoE: An Open Egocentric Manipulation Dataset and Toolchain for Embodied Learning

Open-AoE is an open, community-oriented dataset and toolchain designed for embodied learning from egocentric manipulation videos. arXiv - cs.RO

Never Too Late for Force: Accelerating VLA Post-Training with Reactive Force Injection

Pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies struggle in contact states due to limitations with visual input or minor errors. LIFT introduces a force-aware post-training framework by grafting a reactive action expert onto the existing VLA policy. This system injects real-time 6D end-effector force through a causal force memory and cross-attention mechanism for action refreshment during execution. To handle distribution shift from contact feedback, LIFT incorporates an online DAgger loop training on both offline data and human corrections. Experiments show LIFT surpasses vision-only methods in complex tasks like towel folding and book insertion, confirming the necessity of reactive force modeling.

arXiv - cs.RO

MEMORA: Embodied Action Memory from Egocentric Videos for Reasoning and Planning

Developing long-horizon robot planning requires embodied action memory beyond immediate scene prediction, incorporating past experiences like object changes and prior procedures. MEMORA implements this capability through a formation-consolidation-retrieval lifecycle utilizing four distinct memory stores: Environment, Entity, Activity, and Inferred Knowledge. Online editing updates memory with new observations while offline consolidation abstracts repeated actions into reusable patterns. Testing on EPIC-KITCHENS-100 data demonstrated that full MEMORA significantly improved both memory assessment accuracy (up to 20.5 points) and out-of-distribution planning scores (up to 16.6%). These results confirm that editable, consolidated memory effectively supplies remembered context for robust robot decision-making.

Stochastic Filtering for Quorum Sensing in Robot Swarms under Anonymous Communication

Quorum Sensing (QS) is vital for robot swarm coordination, requiring individuals to accurately estimate the group quorum level through communication. Using anonymous protocols allows for scalability by exchanging local neighbor information without revealing identities. However, these anonymous methods can incorrectly double-count messages, biasing the overall quorum estimates. The study proposes a stochastic filtering protocol inspired by $k$-priority sampling ($\text{AN}^k$) to enhance estimate stability compared to baseline and randomized alternatives. $\text{AN}^k$ successfully stabilizes the estimate by actively filtering the message buffer, though it increases recovery time after errors.

arXiv - cs.RO

DiMaS: Distribution Matching for Steering Vision-Language-Action Models

Flow-matching vision-language-action (VLA) models lack effective fine-grained behavioral control through internal representation manipulation. Existing linear steering techniques fail in the VLA setting because behavioral features are not linearly steerable, despite being linearly decodable. The paper introduces DiMaS, a Distribution-Matching Steering strategy designed specifically for flow-matching VLAs. DiMaS controls behavior by transporting between entire representation distributions rather than shifting along a fixed direction. Experiments demonstrate that this method effectively controls behavior in state-of-the-art VLAs and analyzes the generalizability of behavioral control across diverse tasks.

arXiv - cs.RO

ConFlow: Constraints-Guided Learning with Flow Matching for Motion Generation

Proposes ConFlow, a novel framework for generative robot motion modeling that addresses the limitations of current Flow Matching methods by integrating constraints directly into the training objective. It achieves this by employing differentiable barrier or cost functions to incorporate desired task-specific rules. Additionally, ConFlow replaces the standard Gaussian source distribution with a conditional Gaussian Process for improved specification adherence. The method utilizes infeasible demonstrations as negative supervision to enhance constraint satisfaction without extra expert data. Experiments on two-robot navigation show that ConFlow reduces collision rates and improves trajectory quality compared to standard flow matching baselines.
