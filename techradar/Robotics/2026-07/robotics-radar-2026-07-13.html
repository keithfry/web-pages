AgenticFocus: Object-Preserving Mixed Reality Synthesis from Human FPV Video for Dexterous Humanoid Learning

AgenticFocus is a Mixed Reality synthesis pipeline designed to improve humanoid policy learning from standard first-person human videos. It addresses limitations in current methods by restoring occluded object geometry and reconstructing detailed hand motion. The system retargets these reconstructed motions onto a humanoid body using camera-relative alignment and layered compositing techniques. This process generates high-quality datasets pairing focused visual observations with synchronized robot actions and states. Evaluations show AgenticFocus achieving lower trajectory error and smoother wrist motion compared to existing cross-embodiment baselines.

arXiv - cs.RO

FlowDAgger: Human-in-the-Loop Adaptation of Generative Robot Policies in Latent Space

Generative robot policies show potential but struggle with failure modes outside their training data distribution. Collecting large amounts of new data or using online reinforcement learning is often impractical for quick, safe adaptation. FlowDAgger addresses this by efficiently adapting frozen generative policies using limited human demonstrations in the latent space. The method employs action inversion, mapping expert actions to the corresponding noise required under the base policy via reverse-time integration. This generates supervision for a lightweight latent policy that guides the main model during deployment, achieving rapid skill transfer while maintaining prior behavior.

arXiv - cs.RO

SplatCtrl: Perception-Action Coupling via Gaussian Scene Representations and Reactive Robot Control

SplatCtrl is a novel framework designed for collision-free robotic arm control in unstructured and changing environments. It integrates real-time scene reconstruction using 3D Gaussian Splatting (3D-GS) augmented with a hybrid filtering and relocation strategy. The system derives continuous signed distance functions from the Gaussians to estimate collision probability reliably. These metrics are then incorporated into control barrier functions for safe motion planning. Validation across simulation, physical robots, and human workspaces confirms its effectiveness in dynamic settings.

arXiv - cs.RO

Dec-MARVEL: Decentralized Multi-Agent Exploration without Communication under Budget Constraints

Dec-MARVEL is a decentralized framework enabling multi-UAV exploration without relying on communication, addressing constraints like limited sensing and finite budgets. Robots coordinate solely through incidental observations, using any teammate visible within their field of view as a coordination signal. The system employs a graph-attention actor that integrates local frontier geometry, teammate motion, and budget constraints to select feasible waypoints. Training utilizes phase-conditioned critics and a mixture-based budget curriculum for robust learning. Evaluation across various team sizes and budgets showed Dec-MARVEL achieving top or tied-top exploration rates compared to baselines, with successful sim-to-real transfer demonstrated in physical experiments.

Residual Physics-Informed Neural Networks for High-Fidelity BLDC Motor Modeling

Accurate dynamics modeling of Brushless DC (BLDC) motors is essential for advanced robotic joint control. Researchers propose a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) incorporating a deep residual (ResNet) architecture to model the motor's continuous-time behavior. This network takes voltages and parameters as input to predict all six state variables, ensuring adherence to governing electromechanical and thermal differential equations via a composite loss function. The methodology uses curriculum scheduling to stabilize training. Trained PINN inference demonstrates exceptional speed, achieving latencies of 0.1–22 $\mu$s per query, significantly outpacing traditional ODE solvers for real-time control applications.

arXiv - cs.RO

BeyondSight: Object Permanence for End-to-End Autonomous Driving

Autonomous driving faces challenges in partially observable environments due to occluded actors, which cause current systems to lose track of critical agents. BeyondSight is introduced, a framework that addresses this by decoupling actor existence from immediate observation through persistent actor hypotheses. It propagates temporal actor queries and updates them using observation-conditioned evidence for joint perception, prediction, and planning. To support training, nuScenes-Permanence extends the nuScenes dataset with supervision for unobservable actors. Experiments demonstrate BeyondSight substantially improves reasoning during occlusion, boosting detection mAP from zero to 0.249 and lowering planning error from 0.61 to 0.54 L2avg.

TactiDex: A Real-World Tactile-Guided Benchmark for Human-Like Dexterous Manipulation

Tactile feedback is vital for achieving human-like dexterity during hand-object interaction, surpassing limitations in purely kinematic motion transfer. Researchers introduce TactiDex, a novel real-world benchmark dataset that integrates whole-hand tactile signals with multi-granularity kinematic and object states. The work proposes TactiSkill, a new framework utilizing a tri-component tactile reward function. This structured supervision unifies guidance, human alignment, and contact constraints into one objective for robot learning. Experiments confirm that TactiSkill demonstrates superior performance in both manipulation success and physical realism on single and bimanual tasks.

GenVid2Robot: From Video Generation to Robot Manipulation via Rigid-Geometric Consistency

Generative videos offer motion priors for robotics but lack physical executability due to missing geometric details. GenVid2Robot addresses this with a framework ensuring rigid-geometric consistency when translating video motion to real-world trajectories. It processes an initial RGB-D observation and task instruction by tracking semantic anchors across candidate generated videos. The method verifies if the predicted 2D movement is geometrically consistent with the first frame's anchors using a sparse relative $SE(3)$ model, treating videos as uncertain hypotheses. Ultimately, it generates executable grasp-conditioned trajectories by applying only the geometrically validated motion and includes a depth-compensation module for improved robustness during real-robot execution.
